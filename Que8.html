Restaurant Menu

Veg

  1. Paneer Masala:150rs

    2. Paneer Masala is a popular North Indian curry made with soft cubes of paneer (Indian cottage cheese) cooked in a rich, flavorful tomato and onion gravy. The dish is prepared by sautéing onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and aromatic spices such as turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, and garam masala. Fresh cream or butter is often added to give the curry a smooth, creamy texture and a delicious taste. Paneer Masala is loved for its mildly spicy flavor and is commonly served with naan, roti, paratha, or jeera rice. It is a favorite choice for family meals, festive occasions, and restaurant dining because of its rich taste and satisfying texture.

  2. Veg Biryani:200rs

    3. Veg Biryani is a delicious and aromatic rice dish made with basmati rice, mixed vegetables, and a blend of flavorful Indian spices. It is prepared by cooking vegetables such as carrots, peas, beans, potatoes, and cauliflower with onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and spices like turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala, and biryani masala. The cooked vegetables are layered with fragrant basmati rice and slow-cooked (dum cooked) to allow the flavors to blend perfectly. Veg Biryani is often garnished with fresh coriander, mint leaves, and fried onions, making it even more flavorful and appealing. It is commonly served with raita, salad, or pickle and is a popular choice for family meals, festivals, and special occasions because of its rich taste, aroma, and colorful presentation.

  3. Kaju curry:100rs

    4. Kaju Curry is a rich and creamy North Indian dish made with cashew nuts (kaju) cooked in a flavorful onion and tomato-based gravy. The curry is prepared by sautéing onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and aromatic spices such as turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin, and garam masala. A paste of cashews and fresh cream is often added to make the gravy smooth, thick, and mildly sweet. Whole roasted cashew nuts are then mixed into the gravy, giving the dish a delicious crunchy texture and rich taste. Kaju Curry is commonly garnished with fresh coriander leaves and served hot with butter naan, roti, paratha, or jeera rice. It is a popular choice for festivals, parties, and special occasions because of its luxurious flavor and creamy texture.

Non Veg

  1. Chicken Biryani:

    2. Chicken Biryani is a flavorful rice dish made with basmati rice, tender chicken, yogurt, and aromatic spices. The chicken and rice are cooked together using the traditional dum method, creating a rich aroma and delicious taste. It is commonly served with raita, salad, or boiled eggs.

  2. Chicken Curry:

    3. Chicken Curry is a popular Indian dish prepared by cooking chicken in a spicy onion and tomato-based gravy with ginger, garlic, and traditional Indian spices. It is best enjoyed with rice, roti, naan, or paratha.

  3. Butter Chicken

    4. Butter Chicken is a famous North Indian dish made with grilled or roasted chicken cooked in a creamy tomato and butter gravy. It has a mildly spicy, rich, and slightly sweet flavor and is usually served with naan or jeera rice.

Starters

  • Chicken Tikka:

    Chicken Tikka is made with boneless chicken pieces marinated in yogurt, spices, and herbs, then grilled until tender and smoky. It is served with mint chutney and onion rings.

  • Chicken 65:

    Chicken 65 is a spicy South Indian starter prepared by marinating chicken with spices and deep-frying it until crispy. It is garnished with curry leaves and green chilies.

  • Tandoori Chicken:

    Tandoori Chicken is chicken marinated in yogurt and aromatic spices, then roasted in a tandoor (clay oven). It has a smoky flavor and is served with mint chutney.

  • Chicken Lollipop

    Chicken Lollipop is a popular Indo-Chinese appetizer made from chicken wings coated in spicy batter and deep-fried until crispy. It is served with Schezwan sauce.
